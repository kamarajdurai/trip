Meenakshi Amman Temple

Madurai's crown jewel — 14 towering gopurams covered in 33,000 colorful sculptures. Enter the immersive virtual tour of this 2500-year-old wonder!

South Gopuram
The tallest of all 14 gopurams, rising 51.9 meters! Each gopuram is covered with thousands of brightly painted stucco figures of gods, demons, and mythical creatures. Built in the 16th century by Nayaka kings.
Main Entrance
Welcome to Meenakshi Amman Temple! Dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi (Parvati) and Lord Sundareswarar (Shiva). The temple complex covers 14.5 acres and is visited by 15,000 pilgrims daily.
Porthamarai Kulam (Golden Lotus Tank)
This sacred golden lotus tank is said to test literary merit — legend says that poems were placed here, and only great works would float! The tank glitters beautifully under the golden light.
Hall of 1000 Pillars
Built in 1569 AD, this magnificent hall has 985 ornately carved pillars — each one unique! When tapped, the musical pillars produce different musical notes. A true marvel of ancient engineering.
Main Sanctum — Meenakshi Shrine
The inner sanctum houses Goddess Meenakshi with emerald green skin — representing fertility and prosperity. The fragrance of jasmine flowers and camphor fills this sacred space.
