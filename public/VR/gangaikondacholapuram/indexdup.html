Entrance Gate
Welcome to Gangaikondacholapuram! Built in 1035 AD by Rajendra Chola I after conquering kingdoms up to the Ganges River. This temple celebrates his greatest victories.
Main Mandapam
The Mandapam (hall) features stunning sculptures of Shiva, Vishnu, and celestial beings. Notice the intricate carvings on every pillar — each tells a story from the Puranas.
Nandi Shrine
Facing the sanctum stands Nandi, Lord Shiva's divine vehicle. This Nandi is unique — it stands slightly to the side, as if bowing in reverence to the great Shivalingam inside.
Shikhara Tower (Vimana)
The Vimana rises 55 meters high — slightly shorter than Thanjavur, but equally magnificent. The curving shikhara style was perfected here by Rajendra's master architects.
Temple Gardens
The temple compound spans over 340 acres! The ancient tank called "Ponneri" (Golden Pond) once supplied holy water for rituals. Look around at the serene landscape.
