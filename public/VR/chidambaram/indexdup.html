Temple of the Cosmic Dance

Chidambaram Nataraja Temple

Where Lord Shiva performs the eternal Cosmic Dance (Ananda Tandava) — one of the holiest pilgrimage sites in South India, over 2000 years old!

← Back to All Places
East Gopuram (Raja Gopuram)
The magnificent East Gopuram is the main tower of the temple. It rises over 49 meters and is adorned with golden kalasas (spires) at the top — a stunning sight at sunrise when they glow with golden light.
Kanaka Sabha (Golden Hall)
The Kanaka Sabha (Golden Hall) is the inner sanctum where Nataraja dances eternally. The roof is covered with 21,600 golden tiles representing the 21,600 breaths a human takes per day — a cosmic connection!
Nataraja Sanctum — The Cosmic Dancer
Here Lord Nataraja (Shiva) performs the Ananda Tandava — the Dance of Bliss. The dance represents the cycle of creation, preservation, and dissolution of the universe. One of the most sacred spots in Shaivism!
Sivakami (Parvati) Shrine
Goddess Sivakami — Parvati as Shiva's consort — watches her husband's eternal cosmic dance. Her gentle gaze and graceful form represent divine love and the feminine energy of the universe (Shakti).
Shivaganga Sacred Tank
The Shivaganga tank is one of four sacred tanks inside the temple complex. Pilgrims take a holy dip here before entering the sanctum. The water is believed to have healing properties blessed by Lord Shiva.
